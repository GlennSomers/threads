# Reel Drafts app (web/app)

Scope: the whole app (draft list, draft page, new-idea sheet, Read mode). Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: Glenn alone, on iPhone and Mac equally. Capture ideas fast, send to Claude, read the script while filming.
Constraints: the draft file format is shared with the refine skill and workflow. Single password. No new claims or assets.
Chosen direction: a native Apple app (pinned by Glenn) with the Irish road-sign world lending palette, type weight and one signature move.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A drafts app that looks like it shipped with the iPhone, with one thing no stock app has: the script becomes a road sign you can read from the camera. It refuses the vibe-coded default of cream grounds, orange pills and boxed cards.

OWN-WORLD: Apple system colours and grouped inset lists, SF system font with iOS text styles, hairline separators, blurred bars. Irish national-route green is the tint for every control. Route-number yellow marks spoken length and in-progress states; a yellow warning diamond means refining.

STORY: Glenn opens the app, taps compose, dictates an idea and saves. He taps Refine, sees the diamond, comes back to a script, taps Read and films from the sign.

FIRST VIEWPORT: iPhone: large title "Drafts", segmented control Drafts/Archived, inset grouped list split into Ready to film, Refining and Ideas, bottom toolbar with count and compose. Mac: Notes-style sidebar list on the left, the open draft on the right.

FORM: Native shell (the category canon Glenn chose) fused with the assigned road-sign world; assigned candidate 4 of 7. seed:89b2eae7bc9cdee4

Signature move: Read mode. Full screen sign panel, sign green ground, white inset rounded border, bold white script, yellow route-number chip with the spoken length, b-roll cues in yellow, one size slider, screen kept awake.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
