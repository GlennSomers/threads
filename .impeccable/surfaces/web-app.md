# Reel Drafts app (web/app)

Scope: the whole app (draft list, draft page, new-idea sheet, Read mode). Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: Glenn alone, on iPhone and Mac equally. Capture ideas fast, send to Claude, read the script while filming.
Constraints: the draft file format is shared with the refine skill and workflow. Single password. No new claims or assets.
Chosen direction: a native Apple app in Graphite, picked by Glenn from five colour mockups on 31 Oct 2026. The road-sign world and the sign-green tint were tried and dropped.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A drafts app that looks like it shipped with the iPhone, with no colour at all, like a contact print. The script reads big and plain when he films. It refuses the vibe-coded default of cream grounds, orange pills and boxed cards, and any themed costume.

OWN-WORLD: Apple grouped inset lists, SF system font with iOS text styles, hairline separators, blurred bars. Graphite tint: near-black controls in light mode, near-white in dark. Grey chips for spoken length, a turning open ring for refining, system red only for errors.

STORY: Glenn opens the app, taps compose, dictates an idea and saves. He taps Refine, sees the ring, comes back to a script, taps Read and films from it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: iPhone: large title "Drafts", segmented control Drafts/Archived, inset grouped list split into Ready to Film, Refining and Ideas, bottom toolbar with count and compose. Mac: Notes-style sidebar list on the left, the open draft on the right.

FORM: Native shell, the category canon, in Graphite (the user's pick over the dealt road-sign direction). seed:89b2eae7bc9cdee4

Signature move: Read mode. Full screen, black on white in light mode and white on black in dark, bold script, grey b-roll cues, one size slider, screen kept awake.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
