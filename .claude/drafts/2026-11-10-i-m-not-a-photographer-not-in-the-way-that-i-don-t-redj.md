## Idea

I’m not a photographer, not in the way that I don’t take photos but in the way that it’s not all I do.

Labels are fucking suffocating you as a creative, your outlets are like a venn diagram the more you take away from other outlets the more sections you cut out of your ‘main’ outlet limiting what you can do.

Each avenue of your creativity feeds into each other,
Not only does it give your outlets space and time to breathe but it takes the pressure of you to constantly be creative in that one outlet
