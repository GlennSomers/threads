## Idea

If you shoot less photos, you might shoot less bad photos, but you're definitely going to shoot less good ones. 
The fear of being inadequate or being less than in photography is really what's holding you back.

you just need to be more free-flowing, more creative, more carefree when you're taking your photos.

what I found that helps is taking photos for the joy of capturing, rather than with the end goal of taking a good photo.

Just being present in the moment and capturing the emotions, the memories, the feeling of the moment, and the photo, rather than trying to dictate and curate what I'm actually capturing.
