# Voice Observations (spoken scripts)

Patterns in how Glenn talks in brain dumps and what his choices show. Each line says where it came from. These become the blueprint once there's enough of them.

## From the brain dumps

- Long, run-on spoken sentences that chain with "and", "then", "just because", "despite". The thought keeps moving without full stops. (wedding v2)
- Swears for emphasis at the high points: "Fucking diabolical", "I'm going to fuck up". (wedding v2)
- Talks straight to the people in the story mid-script: "sorry guys". (wedding v2)
- Self-roasting: "Fucking diabolical", "woefully underprepared and under equipped", "stupid confidence". (wedding v2)
- Pairs words for rhythm: "photography and videography", "underprepared and under equipped", "my gear and my skills", "go wrong… make things right". (wedding v2)
- Present tense for the realisation ("I need to accept that I'm going to fuck up"), past tense for the day. (wedding v2)
- Says the turn with "But": "But once I got that all out of my way", "But I definitely could have…". (wedding v2)
- Ends on an open loop to the next video, not a lesson. (wedding v2)
- States his own point plainly when asked: "planning not just for things to go right but even more for things to go wrong", "get multiple people to think of everything you missed". (planning dump)
- Uses "despite" twice. It's his own way to set up a contrast. (wedding v2)

- Wants a promise early that the end pays off, in his own words from the dumps. (decision 23)

- Says "three quarters of an hour", not "45 minutes". (decision 16)
- Picks trims over splits when a line runs long, but takes a split if it lands a joke or a turn ("…15 minutes to get ready. So I snuck in a whiskey"). (decision 16)

- Picks from the line-by-line pop-ups (decision 15): wedding before the risk in the hook; shortest version of a joke; one "fucking" per joke, not two; plain verbs ("snuck in" over "had time to get in"); breaks long lists into two sentences; "a bit of faith… you'll be fine" over "you'll do great".

- His own hand edit (decision 13): trims the setup of a joke so it lands faster ("especially when I never told them that. Sorry guys."), turns the self-roast up ("disgustingly stupid confidence"), adds a throwaway "though" at the end of a joke, uses the right gear words ("readjusting", "hyper directional"), and ends by talking to the viewer.

- Wants scores pushed up, but not at the cost of his voice. Open to more of his character in the script. (decision 7)
