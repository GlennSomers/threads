# Brain dump: wedding series, video 2, the day itself

Date: 2026-10-24
Given after the storytelling review said the script had no scenes from the day.

## Raw dump (verbatim)

Alright here's the brain dump of the day, first off like all weddings everything was delayed squeezing all the time slots together to make up for it, meaning I had to shoot the groom and bride getting ready at the same time, 100's of metres apart. I got my fucking steps in that day I'll tell you that, never fucking doing cardio again. So the shot list went out the window into the fucking pool. But it worked out I got the transitional periods of beginning of getting ready to just about to be done for the bride and thankfully like all men the groom took 15 minutes to get ready so I had time to sneak in a sneaky whiskey even before running back. And that's only one example, I had to keep moving the mic, the tripod for the video camera aka my phone and after all that I'm now finding out how sensitive the pickup pattern on the shotgun mic I had is, so lots of editing to clean it up. Yet everything worked out or is currently working out but definitely better planning and organisation would make it so much smoother and less involved in the editing stage

## Raw dump 3 (verbatim): the planning, given 2026-10-24

I had spare batteries, tested out my phone as the video camera, brought multiple sd cards my all took and an external hard drive. On the day once we found out there were delays I was constantly organising timings for people to start the next stage of the day so I had slotted the groom getting ready 5 minutes into the bridal makeup so I could capture as much as possible. Oh the mic, that's a definite lesson for next time, I should have had a second mic recording a safety track but no that's a painful and time consuming lesson. When planning you'll think of everything you can, so get multiple people to think of everything you missed

The point he wants (his words): "a valuable pay off on planning not just for things to go right but even more for things to go wrong"

## Approved script

Current draft (Glenn's edit, decision 29), not signed off yet:

I shot my friends' wedding despite not touching my camera since February. Fucking diabolical, considering I never told them. Sorry guys.
I knew something would fuck up, so I planned more for things to go wrong than right and went in with disgustingly stupid confidence. And like all weddings, everything's delayed by 45 minutes.
So I'm organising timings, getting the groom to start five minutes into the bridal makeup, running between them, hundreds of metres apart. I got my fucking steps in, never doing cardio again though.
Thankfully, like all men, the groom took 15 minutes to get ready, so I snuck in a whiskey before running back.
What I didn't plan for was how hyper directional the shotgun mic is. I should have had a second mic recording a safety track.
See when you're planning, you'll think of everything you can, so get multiple people to think of everything you missed, especially if you're a bit out of practice.
