# Nathan Perdriau reel: two AI writing tells

Source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd53Kb_G-sN/ (@nathan.perdriau)
Pulled 2026-10-24 to inform Glenn's spoken script voice. Not built into a skill.

## What the reel says

1. Short, staccato sentences stacked back to back give AI away. Natural speech runs long, long, short, then back to long.
2. Use contractions: "can't" not "cannot", "don't" not "do not". AI defaults to the formal version and it sounds robotic.
3. Fix: have Claude check the script for a long, long, short rhythm and collapse every formal contraction.

Note: the caption calls point 2 "contrast negation", but in the video he's talking about contractions. Contrast negation usually means the "it's not X, it's Y" pattern, which the reel doesn't cover.
