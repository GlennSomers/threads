# Book Recs From Reels (Nov 8, 2026)

Pulled from 10 reels (one link was sent twice). Books come from each reel's audio, caption and on-screen covers.
Audible links are the US store (audible.com). "No Audible" means no audiobook edition turned up.

## 1. @seanguderian: books for creatives
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfV_neGA2jg/

- **How to Write One Song** by Jeff Tweedy. The Wilco frontman on getting one idea out and letting it lead to the next. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/How-to-Write-One-Song-Audiobook/0593395670)
- **How to Fail at Almost Everything and Still Win Big** by Scott Adams. The Dilbert creator on systems over goals, and how most of his ideas failed until a few didn't. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/How-to-Fail-at-Almost-Everything-and-Still-Win-Big-Second-Edition-Audiobook/B0DYBW92YF)
- **Stealing Fire** by Steven Kotler and Jamie Wheal (he calls it "How to Steal Fire"). How top performers use flow states to work better. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Stealing-Fire-Audiobook/B01MSMZKM5)
- **The Artist's Way** by Julia Cameron. A 12-week program to unblock creativity, built on daily "morning pages." [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Artists-Way-25th-Anniversary-Edition-Audiobook/0593452526)

## 2. @alekspedia: books for writers
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DePRNAEOLc-/ (sent twice)

- **Dreyer's English** by Benjamin Dreyer. For new writers. Random House's copy chief on clear, correct style. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Dreyers-English-Audiobook/B07LFKH5FV)
- **The Best Punctuation Book, Period** by June Casagrande. For leveling up your commas. A full guide to punctuation across the major style guides. No Audible.
- **Garner's Modern English Usage** by Bryan A. Garner. For people who care about words. A reference on which word or phrasing is right. No Audible.
- **Publication Manual of the APA** (7th ed.). For writing articles. The APA style rules for academic papers. No Audible.
- **The Chicago Manual of Style**. For perfectionists. The full reference for editing and publishing. No Audible.

## 3. @luisbulnes: 7 books for 7 problems
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcndThwtOXX/

- **The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari** by Robin Sharma. If life feels empty. A burned-out lawyer gives up his wealth and learns to live with purpose. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Monk-Who-Sold-His-Ferrari-Audiobook/B071GD6DNJ)
- **Ikigai** by Héctor García and Francesc Miralles. If you can't find your passion. Lessons on purpose and long life from Okinawa. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Ikigai-Audiobook/B074WFYZYH)
- **The War of Art** by Steven Pressfield. If you procrastinate. Names "Resistance" and how to beat it daily. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-War-of-Art-Audiobook/B07PQ9GLDV)
- **Stillness Is the Key** by Ryan Holiday. If you can't switch off. Stoic and Buddhist ideas on calm and focus. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Stillness-Is-the-Key-Audiobook/0593147952)
- **Never Split the Difference** by Chris Voss. For negotiating and hard talks. FBI hostage tactics for everyday deals. He says the audio version is better. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Never-Split-the-Difference-Audiobook/B01CF5O89G)
- **That Will Never Work** by Marc Randolph. If no one believes in your idea. Netflix's co-founder on how it started. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/That-Will-Never-Work-Audiobook/1549121669)
- **Rewire** by Nicole Vignola. To break old patterns. A neuroscientist on changing habits and thought loops. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Rewire-Audiobook/B0CJG25M5H)

## 4. @bryanlowwww: 7 books for 7 problems
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddv-lWovPhh/

- **The Decision Book** by Mikael Krogerus and Roman Tschäppeler. If you struggle to decide. 50 quick models for strategic thinking. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Decision-Book-Audiobook/B07F1Y38Q6)
- **Why We Sleep** by Matthew Walker. If you're always tired. How sleep drives memory, energy, mood and health. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Why-We-Sleep-Audiobook/B0752ZQR33)
- **The Book of Ichigo Ichie** by Héctor García and Francesc Miralles. If you keep waiting for life to get better. The Japanese idea of treating each moment as once in a lifetime. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Book-of-Ichigo-Ichie-Audiobook/0593168763)
- **Love for Imperfect Things** by Haemin Sunim. If you never feel good enough. A Zen monk on self-compassion. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Love-for-Imperfect-Things-Audiobook/0525641165)
- **No Hard Feelings** by Liz Fosslien and Mollie West Duffy. If emotions affect your work. How to use emotions at work instead of hiding them. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/No-Hard-Feelings-Audiobook/0525642072)
- **The Power of Understanding People** by Dave Mitchell. If people confuse you. How to read personality types and talk to each one. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Power-of-Understanding-People-Audiobook/1663710082)
- **Guardians of Being** by Eckhart Tolle and Patrick McDonnell. If you take life too seriously. An illustrated book using cats and dogs to teach presence. No Audible.

## 5. @noahfleming: standing out and building a reputation
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/De32svKtlSv/

- **Stand Out** by Dorie Clark. How to become the go-to person in your field. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Stand-Out-Audiobook/B0FJ2VFTFS)
- **Reinventing You** by Dorie Clark. How to change how people see you after you've moved on. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Reinventing-You-Audiobook/B00C313QVY)
- **Purple Cow** by Seth Godin. Playing it safe keeps you invisible. Be remarkable. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Purple-Cow-Audiobook/B002V0QOJS)
- **How to Get a Meeting with Anyone** by Stu Heinecke. Creative "contact campaigns" to reach busy, hard-to-reach people. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/How-to-Get-a-Meeting-with-Anyone-Audiobook/B01AKJJOMA)
- **The Ultimate Sales Machine** by Chet Holmes. Do a few key things over and over until you're great at them. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Ultimate-Sales-Machine-Audiobook/B002V1O6KQ)
- **Building a StoryBrand** by Donald Miller. Make what you do clear in one sentence, with you as the guide and the customer as the hero. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Building-a-StoryBrand-20-Audiobook/B0CZ59BB9B)
- **Million Dollar Consulting** by Alan Weiss. His top pick. How consultants charge on value and get paid well. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Million-Dollar-Consulting-Sixth-Edition-Audiobook/B0BZ5VPSK5)

## 6. @itsfinanceghost: marketing "degree"
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeP3gJAJ2CM/

- **$100M Leads** by Alex Hormozi. "Freshman year." How to get strangers to know you exist and become leads. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/100M-Leads-Audiobook/B0C7WJXBRL)

## 7. @billyloveofficial: Dostoevsky's big five, in reading order
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dalm-uZJLD5/

He recommends the Pevear and Volokhonsky translations. Links below are that translation where one exists.

- **Crime and Punishment**. A poor student kills a pawnbroker and his conscience eats him alive. [Audible (P&V)](https://www.audible.com/pd/Crime-and-Punishment-Audiobook/B0DTB4QJMW)
- **Notes from Underground**. About 130 pages. A bitter recluse on why people act against their own interest. [Audible (P&V)](https://www.audible.com/pd/Notes-from-Underground-Audiobook/B0GTRWXZQ6)
- **Demons**. 700+ pages. A town torn apart by radical revolutionaries. No P&V audiobook found. [Audible (other translation)](https://www.audible.com/pd/Demons-Audiobook/B0CZ4NKKWX)
- **The Idiot**. A truly good man meets a corrupt society. He calls the ending bone-chilling. [Audible (P&V)](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Idiot-Audiobook/B0GFP7P2S7)
- **The Brothers Karamazov**. His favorite book ever. Three brothers, a murdered father, faith and doubt. Includes "The Grand Inquisitor." [Audible (P&V)](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Brothers-Karamazov-Bicentennial-Edition-Audiobook/B0CKM7JKD1)

## 8. @nolan_killeen: five life-changing books
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Db9Oql2Iiqv/

- **The Way of the Fight** by Georges St-Pierre. Bullied kid to MMA great. Turning fear into fuel. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Way-of-the-Fight-Audiobook/B00C252V8A)
- **Stumbling on Happiness** by Daniel Gilbert. Humans are bad at predicting what will make them happy. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Stumbling-on-Happiness-Audiobook/B002V5BAQU)
- **The Metamorphosis** by Franz Kafka. A man turns into a bug, and his family stops caring once he can't earn. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Metamorphosis-Audiobook/B00MV4Q3JA)
- **The Sickness Unto Death** by Søren Kierkegaard. Most people are in despair and don't know it. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Sickness-Unto-Death-Audiobook/B0CT5DPFW3)
- **Nicomachean Ethics** by Aristotle. His pick for most important. Virtue is built by habit. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Nicomachean-Ethics-Audiobook/B004TC0TSS)

## 9. @williampdozier: one book for writing
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddr_JiCJ8Te/

- **How Fiction Works** by James Wood. A critic breaks down the craft of fiction: point of view, detail, character, voice. Makes you a better reader and writer. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/How-Fiction-Works-Audiobook/B002V0K9NU)

## 10. @_ethanbricker: four books for writers
Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfHuZfgxc9n/

- **Steal Like an Artist** by Austin Kleon. Nothing is fully original. How to take what inspires you and make it your own without copying. Short, easy to flip through. No standalone audiobook. It's in Kleon's own reading of [The Steal Like an Artist Audio Trilogy](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Steal-Like-an-Artist-Audio-Trilogy-Audiobook/B0B6QGTNVS), with Show Your Work and Keep Going.
- **On Writing Well** by William Zinsser. The Yale writing course in a book. What to do and avoid, with chapters for each type of writing: memoir, sports, science and more. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/On-Writing-Well-Audio-Collection-Audiobook/B002V8KQLC) (abridged, read by Zinsser)
- **Poems That Touch the Heart** compiled by A.L. Alexander. A collection of popular inspirational poems. He says thinking like a poet made him a better writer. No Audible.
- **A Rulebook for Arguments** by Anthony Weston. Short rules for building strong arguments and spotting fallacies and weak reasoning. [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/A-Rulebook-for-Arguments-Audiobook/B0DD9L9MJ6)
